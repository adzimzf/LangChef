## です/ます

Polite copula and verb ending. Used in formal/polite speech.

- **です** follows nouns and na-adjectives
- **ます** follows verb stems

> Example: 学生です / 食べます

---

## て-form

Connects verbs in sequence or makes requests.

- `V-te + ください` = please do
- `V-te + います` = ongoing action/state

> Example: 食べてください

---

## たい

Attaches to verb stem to express desire: "want to do".

> Example: 食べたい = I want to eat.

---

## ある/いる

Existence verbs.

- **ある** for inanimate objects
- **いる** for animate beings (people, animals)

---

## ましょう

Volitional polite form: "Let's do".

Also used as a polite suggestion or invitation.
