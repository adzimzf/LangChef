## Sedang + verb

Progressive: "is doing". Marks ongoing action.

> Example: Sedang makan = is eating

---

## Sudah + verb

Perfective: "already did". Also used as a question.

> Example: Sudah makan? = Have you eaten?

---

## Belum + verb

Not yet: "haven't done". Opposite of sudah.

> Example: Belum makan = haven't eaten yet

---

## Mau + verb

Future intention: "want to / about to".

> Example: Mau pergi = about to go

---

## Bisa / Boleh

Ability and permission:

- **Bisa** = can/able to (ability)
- **Boleh** = may/allowed to (permission)

> Example: Bisa berenang = can swim
